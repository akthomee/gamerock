// Common Reasons

What people usually write in about

Broken download link

File hosts rotate, expire, and occasionally vanish. If a download button sends you to a dead page or a "file not found" screen, tell us which page it was on. Include the game or app name — we'll find a working mirror and update it within a day or two.

Game or app not working

If you followed the install instructions and something went wrong — crash on launch, black screen, "app not installed" error — describe what happened and include your Android version and phone model. We've almost certainly seen the issue before, and a quick tweak to the instructions usually fixes it for everyone.

Request a game or app

Want us to cover something specific? Tell us the title and, if you know it, the developer. We prioritise requests that fit the site — Android games, modding tools, media apps. If it's available on Google Play and not region-locked, we'll likely add it.

Corrections & typos

Spotted a wrong version number, an outdated spec, or a sentence that reads badly? Send the page link and what you noticed. Small corrections keep the site useful.

DMCA / takedown requests

If you own the rights to a game, app, or other content referenced on GameRock and want it removed, email us with the details. We respond to legitimate takedown requests promptly. See our Terms of Service for the full policy on third-party content and intellectual property.

Advertising & partnerships

For ad placement questions, sponsorships, or collaboration requests, email us with the subject line "Partnership" and a short description of what you're proposing.

Anything else

Not sure which category fits? Just write. We read everything that arrives, even if the reply takes a day or two.

// Before You Write

Two quick things that help

  • Include the page link. If your message is about a specific game, app, or tutorial, paste the URL. It saves us digging through the site to figure out which one you mean.
  • Tell us what you've tried. For technical issues, "it doesn't work" is hard to act on. "It crashes after the loading screen on Android 13" is something we can fix.
We are not the developers. GameRock is a directory and knowledge resource. We don't own the games or apps, and we can't fix in-game bugs, restore lost accounts, or refund in-app purchases. Those go to the actual studios — Gameloft, Mojang, CarX Technologies, etc.
// Response

How fast we reply

Most messages get a reply within 24 hours. WhatsApp is the fastest channel — usually a few hours. Email is checked daily. If your message needs research (broken mirror, working alternative for an old game), expect a follow-up a day or two later once we've had a chance to dig.

We don't use an auto-responder and we don't send marketing emails. When you hear back from us, it's a real reply from a real person.
