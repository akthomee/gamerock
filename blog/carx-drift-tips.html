CarX Street: 7 Drift Tips That Actually Work

Stop crashing into the guardrail. Whether you're driving a starter car or a fully tuned supercar, these 7 tips will fix your suspension, master the handbrake, and help you hold long, clean drifts.

CarX Street isn't like other arcade racers. In games like Asphalt, drifting is automatic. You tap the brake, the car slides, and the game holds your hand to keep you on the track. CarX Street is different. It uses a realistic physics engine, which means if you don't know how to transfer the weight of the car, you're going to spin out and hit a wall.

But once you figure it out? It's the most satisfying racing experience on mobile. Holding a perfect drift through a tight corner, smoke pouring off the tires, while you chain together a combo multiplier—it feels incredible.

If you're struggling to keep your car sideways, here are the 7 tips that will immediately improve your drifting in CarX Street.

1. Switch to Manual Transmission (If You Can)

The biggest mistake beginners make is leaving the car in Automatic. In Automatic mode, the game decides when to shift gears. If you're mid-drift and the car suddenly shifts up, you lose RPM and your drift dies.

Switch to Manual transmission in the settings. It feels overwhelming at first, but you need control over your gears to drift properly. When you enter a corner, drop down a gear (or two) to keep your RPM high. That high rev range is what keeps the wheels spinning and the car sliding.

2. Don't Use the Handbrake to Start the Drift

When you watch real-life drifting, drivers yank the handbrake. In CarX Street, doing this at high speed will usually just make you spin out 180 degrees.

Instead of the handbrake, use the Weight Transfer technique. As you approach the corner, flick the steering wheel in the opposite direction for a split second (e.g., steer right just before turning left). This shifts the weight of the car to the outside tires. Then, turn hard into the corner. The back end will naturally kick out.

3. Throttle Control is Everything

Once the car is sideways, your instinct is to hold the gas pedal all the way down. Don't do that. If you mash the throttle, the rear tires will gain too much grip, and you'll straighten out and crash.

You need to feather the throttle. Tap it in and out. As the car starts to straighten out, give it more gas to kick the back end out again. It's a constant balancing act of steering and tapping the gas.

4. Tune Your Suspension (Hard Rear, Soft Front)

If you are constantly spinning out, your car's setup is wrong. Go to the garage and look at your suspension tuning.

  • Rear Suspension: Make it stiffer (Harder). A stiff rear end prevents the car from rolling too much and helps the tires break traction quickly.
  • Front Suspension: Soften it slightly. This helps the front tires maintain grip so you can actually steer while the back end is sliding.
  • Tire Pressure: Increase the rear tire pressure slightly. Higher pressure means less grip, which makes drifting easier.

5. Look Where You Want to Go

This is a real-world driving rule that applies perfectly to CarX. When you are sliding sideways, the wall is coming at you fast. Your instinct is to stare at the wall. If you stare at the wall, you will hit the wall.

Keep your eyes on the exit of the corner. Your thumbs will naturally follow your eyes. Counter-steer (turn the wheel opposite to the direction of the slide) to keep the car pointed towards the exit.

6. Upgrade Your Tires First

When you start the game, you have a limited budget. What should you upgrade first? Engine? Turbo? No—upgrade your Tires.

Buy the best Sports or Drift tires you can afford. A high-grip tire makes the car predictable. Cheap tires will randomly lose grip on smooth asphalt, making it impossible to hold a clean line. Once you have good tires, then you can start upgrading the engine for more power.

7. Practice in the Industrial Zone

Don't practice drifting in the crowded city center. The traffic and tight alleys will just frustrate you.

Drive out to the industrial zone or the mountain roads on the edge of the map. These areas have wide, sweeping corners with plenty of runoff space. You can mess up, spin out, and try again without getting a "busted" screen or crashing into an NPC car. Spend an hour just doing figure-eights in an empty parking lot. It builds muscle memory.

Summary: Practice Makes Perfect

CarX Street is a game of patience. You aren't going to become a drift god in your first hour. But if you turn off auto-transmission, stop yanking the handbrake, and tune your suspension properly, you'll notice a massive difference in your driving.

Get out there, find an empty lot, and start practicing. Once you hit that perfect drift combo, you'll understand why CarX has the most dedicated racing community on mobile.
