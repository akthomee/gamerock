// About

What this app is about

This app can search for any music and have it downloaded. It can also download any video on YouTube — you just have to search the video's title. You can view your downloads within and outside the app with any music or video player.

What's most fun about this app? It's absolutely FREE. I threw away Boomplay and Audiomack for this baby.

Install instructions

  1. Download the APK using the button above.
  2. Ignore any redirects and pop-ups — use the back button to return to the download page.
  3. Complete the captcha countdown and proceed to the generated download link.
  4. Enable Install from unknown sources in your device settings.
  5. Install and open Free Music Downloader.
  6. Search any song or video title, tap download, and it saves to your device.
  7. Play the file inside the app or point your favourite music player at the download folder.
