// Overview

Which media app should you pick?

Each media app solves a different problem. Here's a quick decision guide:

  • BlackHole — best when you want a quick video or audio grab from a social link. Paste and download. Handles any platform.
  • FzStudioX — best for full movies, TV series and anime. Unified search across sources, plays in any external player.
  • Free Music Downloader — best for songs and YouTube audio. Search by title, tap download, play offline.

All three are free, ad-free, and let you keep what you download. Pick based on what you're grabbing — video, movie, or music.
