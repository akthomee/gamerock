// Our Story

Why GameRock exists

GameRock started as a personal frustration. Finding a working Android game — especially the classics Gameloft pulled from the Play Store — meant bouncing between dead links, ad-infested mirror sites, and forums where half the download links had expired years ago. And when you finally did find a file, there was no guide telling you how to install it, which virtual machine you needed, or what folder the data went in.

So we built the site we wished existed. A clean, fast, no-nonsense directory that doesn't waste your time, and a place that actually teaches you how to install, configure, and get the most out of the software you download.

// Our Approach

How we handle downloads

GameRock is a directory and knowledge resource. We publish descriptions, installation guides, tutorials, blog posts, and curated articles to help users understand and safely use the software they're interested in.

We also provide links to third-party file hosts where software may be available. We do not host, develop, or distribute the software files themselves. All game titles, apps, logos, screenshots and trademarks referenced on this site belong to their respective owners.

Every install guide is written by someone who has actually installed the thing. When we say "run it inside a virtual machine," it's because we ran into the same Android version errors you would. When we say "launch GameGuardian before the game," it's because we learned that the hard way.

Always scan before you install. Any APK you get from a third-party host — here or anywhere else — should be scanned with up-to-date antivirus software before installation. We link out, we don't verify.
// Principles

What we stand for

No clutter, no bait

We don't gate downloads behind fake surveys, we don't stack our pages with five autoplay videos, and we don't write clickbait titles. What you came for is on the page, in front of you.

Teach, don't just link

Every game and app page has a specs table, a description of what it actually does, and step-by-step install instructions. If there's a tutorial that makes the experience better — like the N.O.V.A Legacy coinfarming guide — we write it and link it from the game page.

Free stays free

Everything on GameRock is free to access. Donations through TON or Naira keep the lights on, but nothing is behind a paywall, and no download requires you to subscribe to anything.

Say what we are

We're a directory. We link to third-party hosts. We don't own the games, we don't develop them, and we're not affiliated with the studios that do — Gameloft, Mojang, CarX Technologies or anyone else. Our Terms of Service spell this out in full.
