// About

What kind of game is Minecraft?

Minecraft is a sandbox game — and as the name says, it's "mine-craft": you get items, then try to make something good with them. Mining/sourcing, then crafting.

Minecraft allows you to explore and blow your creativity without bounds. The game permits you to see yourself in different professions as you wish — farmer, fisherman, traveller, cave explorer, builder and a lot more. Interact with animals, mobs and villagers in the game and encounter endless fights with the hostile mobs in survival mode. The game has two major game modes: creative and survival.

In the creative game mode, you are free to work with unlimited resources and thus craft as far as your mind can reach — and perhaps perform your experiments.

In the survival mode, you have a health and hunger bar. You have to play hard to get food, shelter and items — mine for your ores, fish, craft weapons, trade with villagers, fight hostile mobs, explore for shipwrecks and lost treasures. Do everything you can to survive!

The game has offline and online modes. Join the servers and realms to play games with thousands of other players like you — but don't get carried away by the mind-blowing architectures. Among the things you do in this game is building a portal to hell and fighting the most aggressive creatures in a hell-terraformed dimension. The basic aim of every survival player is to defeat the ENDER DRAGON!
