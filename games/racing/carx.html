CarX Street

Open-world drift racing for Android and iOS. Deep customization, realistic physics, day/night cycle and multiplayer.

Latest Version
// About

Story & gameplay

CarX Drift Racing: Street (commonly referred to as CarX Street) is an open-world racing game developed by CarX Technologies, blending high-speed street racing with deep customization and realistic driving mechanics. It's designed for fans of arcade racing and car enthusiasts who enjoy immersive gameplay and competitive racing dynamics.

Warning. You may spend hours playing this game. Be sure to take periodic breaks.
// Features

Open-world exploration

Players can freely roam a sprawling urban environment featuring highways, city streets, and winding mountain roads — providing a diverse landscape for racing and exploration. This expansive setting encourages players to discover hidden shortcuts, secret locations, and unique drift zones that enhance their racing strategies and overall experience. The freedom to navigate through various terrains — from bustling downtown areas to serene countryside routes — adds depth to the gameplay, allowing players to engage in spontaneous races or simply enjoy the scenery.

The world includes a dynamic day-night cycle, adding visual variety and challenges to races. As the sun sets and the city lights begin to twinkle, players must adapt to changing visibility conditions and altered road dynamics. Nighttime racing introduces new elements, such as illuminated streets and neon signs, creating a vibrant atmosphere that enhances the thrill of competition. Additionally, weather effects can also play a role, influencing traction and handling, which further tests players' skills and decision-making in real-time. This immersive environment ensures that no two races are ever the same, keeping the gameplay fresh and exciting.

// Features

Intense racing gameplay

Offers various modes, including street racing, drift challenges, and time trials — catering to a wide range of racing preferences and skill levels. Players can engage in high-stakes street races against AI competitors or friends, showcasing their speed and strategy. Drift challenges allow players to hone their skills in precision driving, encouraging them to master the art of drifting around corners while earning points for style and technique. Time trials add an element of competition against the clock, pushing players to achieve their best times on meticulously designed tracks. Each mode presents unique challenges and rewards, ensuring that players remain engaged and motivated to improve their racing prowess.

Realistic driving physics and responsive controls provide an authentic racing experience that immerses players in the thrill of high-speed competition. The game's physics engine accurately simulates vehicle dynamics, allowing players to feel the weight and handling of their cars as they navigate sharp turns and accelerate down straightaways. This attention to detail enhances the overall gameplay, as players must adapt their driving techniques based on their vehicle's characteristics and the conditions of the track. The responsive controls ensure that every input feels intuitive and precise, enabling players to execute complex maneuvers with confidence. This combination of realism and responsiveness not only heightens the excitement of each race but also fosters a deeper connection between the player and their vehicle, making every victory feel truly earned.

// Features

Diverse car collection

The game features a wide range of vehicles, from classic sports cars to modern supercars, each with unique handling characteristics that cater to different racing styles. Players can select from an extensive lineup of iconic models, allowing them to experience the charm of vintage cars alongside the cutting-edge performance of today's supercars.

Each vehicle is meticulously designed to reflect its real-world counterpart, with distinct driving dynamics that enhance the gameplay. Whether it's the nimble handling of a lightweight coupe or the brute force of a muscle car, players can experiment to find the perfect fit for their racing style.

The game also encourages players to build their dream garage by unlocking new vehicles through achievements or in-game currency, adding an element of progression and excitement. With regular updates introducing new models, the collection remains fresh, providing endless opportunities for exploration and competition in the world of automotive performance.

// Features

Online multiplayer

Players can compete against others in real-time multiplayer modes, showcasing their driving skills and customized cars. This dynamic environment fosters a competitive spirit as racers from around the world challenge each other on various tracks, creating thrilling head-to-head matchups. Additionally, players can join or create racing clubs, allowing them to team up with friends or like-minded enthusiasts for cooperative events and tournaments, further enhancing the social aspect of the game. With leaderboards tracking performance and achievements, players are motivated to improve their skills and climb the ranks, making every race an opportunity for glory.
