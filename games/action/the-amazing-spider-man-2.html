// About

A Parallel Story in the Spider-Verse

Unlike the first game, which retold the movie's plot, The Amazing Spider-Man 2 for Android tells a story that runs alongside the film. Peter Parker is juggling his life as a high school graduate with his responsibilities as New York's protector. But when a new threat emerges—a powerful being named Electro—Peter is forced to confront enemies far more dangerous than he's ever faced before.

The game introduces new characters from the film, including Electro and Harry Osborn, but it also weaves in original side stories and villains that expand the universe. You'll explore a fully realized Manhattan, from the neon lights of Times Square to the quiet corners of Central Park, and uncover clues about Oscorp's sinister experiments.

// Features

Open-World Swinging & Combat

The web-swinging in this game is fluid and fast. You can swing between skyscrapers, run up walls, and use a "Web Rush" mechanic to zip to specific points with precision. The city feels alive, with random crimes to stop and civilians to rescue as you make your way through the story.

Combat blends melee strikes, web attacks, and counters. You can unlock new suits and upgrade your abilities as you progress, giving you more ways to take down enemies. Boss fights against villains like Electro and the Green Goblin are challenging and cinematic, requiring you to use all your spider-powers.
