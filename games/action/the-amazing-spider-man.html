// About

Story & gameplay

Before Marvel's Spider-Man on PS4, Gameloft gave us the definitive open-world Spider-Man experience on mobile. The Amazing Spider-Man was released alongside the 2012 movie and featured a fully explorable 3D Manhattan.

The story serves as a sequel to the movie. Peter Parker is struggling to balance his life as a high school student with his responsibilities as Spider-Man. Meanwhile, a new threat emerges as Oscorp's illegal cross-species experiments go wrong, unleashing the Lizard and other villains across the city.

The gameplay is a mix of free-roaming web-swinging and intense indoor brawls. You can seamlessly transition from swinging between skyscrapers to diving into a rooftop fight. The combat system is fast and fluid, allowing you to dodge, web-strike, and perform acrobatic takedowns.

// Features

A True Open-World Experience

The map is massive for a mobile game. You can swing anywhere, from Times Square to Central Park. There are hundreds of collectibles, side missions, and random crimes to stop. The web-swinging mechanics feel fantastic, allowing you to build momentum and run up the sides of buildings.

It also includes the "Web Rush" mechanic, which lets you slow down time, pick a specific target (like a ledge or an enemy), and zip directly to it with a press of a button. It makes you feel like a superhero.
