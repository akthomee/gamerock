Story & gameplay

Released in 2010, Modern Combat 2: Black Pegasus was a massive leap forward for mobile shooters. Gameloft took the foundation of the first game and polished it into a cinematic, console-like experience.

The story picks up right where the first game left off. A new terrorist organization has emerged, threatening global stability. You step into the boots of different soldiers from the elite Black Pegasus squad, fighting through 12 intense missions across the Middle East, South America, and Europe.

The gameplay is classic run-and-gun FPS action. You'll use a variety of assault rifles, shotguns, and sniper rifles to take down enemy forces. The controls were specifically designed for touchscreens, featuring an auto-aim system and customizable layouts that made it easy to jump right into the action.

// Features

Why Black Pegasus is a Classic

Unlike modern mobile shooters that are filled with loot boxes, battle passes, and forced online matchmaking, Modern Combat 2 is a pure, offline, single-player experience. You buy the game (or download the APK), and you get the entire campaign with zero microtransactions.

It also featured a surprisingly deep multiplayer mode back in the day. While those servers are long gone, the single-player campaign remains one of the best in the history of mobile gaming. It's a piece of history that still holds up today.
