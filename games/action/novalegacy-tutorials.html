N.O.V.A Legacy Tutorials

Nine practical tips and tricks that will make you progress faster — from coinfarming to camping to doubling your packs.

9 Tips
// The Playbook

Nine things every N.O.V.A Legacy player should know

01 How to get maxed out fast

One can get maxed out in less than 4 months by coinfarming, playing multiplayer and events. Coinfarming generates more coins, events return valuable cards, and multiplayer returns a few rewards.

02 What is coinfarming and how to do it

Coinfarming is the process of getting coins faster in the game. It's done by using rocket launchers (advisably two) and going to the first mission of New Ceres Jungle to kill the enemies from the heliport. By doing this, you'll obtain an RPG kill (+50 coins) for each successful shot.

For coinfarming to be successful, it's advisable to use Boom and Ignition — Boom has the largest magazine size, followed by Ignition. Also, upgrade Boom to at least 220 and Ignition to 170. All from experience.

03 What gear combination to use

If you want to get maxed out on time, you're advised to use gears from epic cards. The easiest combination is Sagittarius and Juggernaut. Also, snipers are the most powerful weapons. If you want to move faster in-game, use Cheetah or Nightcore — Cheetah is easier to max out, just like Juggernaut. However, the most powerful and reliable combination is Arachnid and Titan, or Nightcore.

Here's a video on gear combination.

04 Events are tough — here's the trick

Events are meant to be tough, but as a noob the pros won't let you see the light. To make things better, start events in the last six hours — or the last four hours, or even the final 1 day 1 hour. There was a time I started Rush Elite with 30 minutes left and got ten packs. Just know your event server is random.

05 What avatar to use

It's fine to use any avatar, but some have special specs. Prometheus's spec is invisibility — usually in Alien City. Ludo's spec is that his head is sometimes hard to hit. One advantage of collecting cards to unlock avatars is the coins generated from the excess.

06 How to camp

Camping requires skill and experience. As a beginner, find a favourite spot usually close to a spawn point and practice with it. You can have many favourite spots. You can also deal with campers by attacking from their blind spots, or using a katana or even an RPG. Remember: snipers are a camper's weapon of choice.

07 How to gain ratings

Ratings are gained when you take first position in a match with the most kills and the least deaths — especially against players who already have a good number of ratings.

08 How to double packs

Say you have 20 packs and want them doubled. Open one pack, then turn off your mobile data or Wi-Fi (whichever you're using). After that, open all the packs and wait at least 1 minute — 2 minutes is advisable — then tap Open. Magically, you'll be able to open another 19 packs.

Here's a video on how to double packs.

09 How to change game server

There are two servers in this game: European and Asian. Europe, Africa and some other regions default to the European server, while Asian countries default to the Asian server. To change your server, delete the game files and connect via VPN to a country that has the server you want — e.g. America for the European server, India for the Asian server. Then open the game and go online.
