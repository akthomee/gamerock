// About

Realistic aerial combat

Sky Combat is an aircraft combat game built on real physical aircraft parameters and physics. There's a variety of fighter jets from the MiG-19 to the fifth-generation Su-57. The game is flexible enough to let you battle from the skies to the valleys to the sea tops, with realistic physics and very good graphics.

Sky Combat is available for iOS and Android. Only the best pilots make the wins.

// Features

What's inside

Battle in different maps

Battle in 3 maps — Mountains, City, and Arctic. The different maps give players wonderful experiences as they bomb aircraft carriers, destroy artillery, and fight within the environment with optimum flexibility.

A variety of fighter jets

Pilot fighter jets from the 1980s to fifth-generation fighters.

A variety of fire power

There's a lot of missiles and aircraft guns in this game. Among the unguided missiles you have the LAU-3 up to the Air-2 Genie, and among the guided missiles the AIM-120D and R-27R. Aircraft guns really do matter in this game.
