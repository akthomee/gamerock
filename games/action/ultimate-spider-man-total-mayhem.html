// About

Story & gameplay

Before the Marvel Cinematic Universe took over the world, Gameloft gave us Ultimate Spider-Man: Total Mayhem. Released in 2010, it was a console-quality action game that somehow fit into your pocket.

The story is simple: a new formula has hit the streets of New York, turning ordinary thugs into super-powered monsters. It’s up to Peter Parker to swing into action, beat the living daylights out of the bad guys, and figure out who is behind the chaos.

The game features 12 iconic Spider-Man villains, including Venom, Green Goblin, Sandman, and Rhino. The combat system is incredibly fluid, allowing you to chain together punches, web-strikes, and aerial combos. It’s not just a web-swinging simulator; it’s a hardcore beat-'em-up.

// Features

Fluid Combat & Web-Swinging

The web-swinging mechanics were groundbreaking for mobile at the time. You can sling from building to building with a simple swipe, and the game automatically locks onto targets so you can dive-kick enemies from above.

The combat system rewards you for style. As you land hits without taking damage, your combo meter builds up, allowing you to unleash devastating special moves and take down multiple enemies at once.
