// About

Story & gameplay

Released in 2012, Modern Combat 4: Zero Hour is widely considered the greatest game Gameloft ever made. It pushed mobile hardware to its breaking point, delivering a first-person shooter experience that rivaled console games.

The story is a massive step up from previous entries. Terrorists have kidnapped the President of the United States, and the world is on the brink of nuclear war. What makes this campaign unique is that you don't just play as the elite soldier, Sgt. James Walker. You also play as the main antagonist, Edward Page, giving you a terrifying perspective on the villain's motivations.

The gameplay features intense firefights, stealth sections, and vehicle sequences. You can lean around corners, throw drones to scan areas, and use a massive arsenal of modern weaponry. The controls are heavily refined and feel incredibly smooth.

// Features

The Best Campaign on Mobile

Unlike the later games in the series (which became free-to-play and heavily monetized), Modern Combat 4 is a premium, offline experience. There are no energy timers, no loot boxes, and no forced internet connection. You get a full, cinematic campaign with incredible voice acting and set pieces.

The graphics are still stunning today. The lighting effects, the weapon models, and the destruction physics were years ahead of their time. If you want to experience the golden era of mobile shooters, this is the definitive title.
